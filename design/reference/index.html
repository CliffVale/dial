Dial reference previews

Interactive, browser-only references for the native app. Arrow keys turn, Space presses, D double presses, trackpad scroll also turns. Add ?theme=light or ?theme=dark to force a theme.

  • Dial: ring, knob, turn and press states
  • MenuBarDrop: the full loop from the menu bar
  • ModeHUD: the pill for levels, steps and lists
  • AppFan: Apps mode card fan
  • MissionControl: window spread and selection
